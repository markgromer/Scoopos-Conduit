# Bow Wow: Build Your First AI Employee

Tomorrow's session is hands-on. We're going beyond prompts and building a business agent with an actual job.

Your mission: teach an AI agent what your company does, give it a repeatable workflow, decide which business apps it can use, and test what it can do.

**Start here:** https://scoopos-agent-lab.markgromer.workers.dev/

Bring ChatGPT on a phone or laptop. You don't need to know how to code. If your account supports Work and connected plugins, we'll use them to test a real business task. If not, you'll still build a persistent Project and learn exactly which capabilities require a connected tool or a different plan.

You'll leave with your own instructions, a knowledge checklist, a test plan, and an agent you can continue improving. We won't count a generated prompt as a finished deployment: testing against a real approved source is the goal.

**Class materials / developer extension:** https://github.com/markgromer/Scoopos-Conduit/tree/main/workshops/2026-10-12-build-an-agent

No real customer information in the public workshop. No auto-sending or autonomous billing. We'll build the parts that actually work, and be clear about what still requires another integration.
