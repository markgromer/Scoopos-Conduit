# Next level: only after the basic habit works

You now have a **manual, visible lead process**. Here's a sensible upgrade order.

1. **Prove the workflow:** every lead has a stage, owner, permitted contact method, and next action.
2. **Connected reads:** if an approved Airtable ChatGPT connector is available, verify that the assistant reads exact records.
3. **Approved writes:** show each proposed field change; record the authorized write result and verify the record.
4. **Alerts:** notify an assigned human when an eligible lead needs action; don't silently start an unsolicited outbound campaign.
5. **Inbound integrations:** later connect Facebook lead forms, website forms, Quo, or CRM webhooks through tested, authenticated services.
6. **Nurture:** only when contact permission, suppression/opt-out, quiet-hour rules, retry limits, and an audit trail are enforced.
7. **Booking/quotes/payment:** connect to your actual scheduling and billing source of truth; never fabricate availability or payment success.

### Important distinction

**LeadBridge is planned but not shipped here.** This package has no live Make.com bridge, Meta webhook, SMS automation, CRM synchronization, or Stripe integration. Do not treat the fictional rows as live leads.

**Airtable connection ≠ automation.** An interactive ChatGPT session may support reading or authorized edits when the plugin is connected. Unattended automation is a separate build with separate permissions and reliability requirements.
