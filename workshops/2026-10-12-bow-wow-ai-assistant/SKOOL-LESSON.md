# Lesson: Build Your First AI Business Assistant

**This week's Bow Wow workshop:** Stop chatting with AI and give it a real job.

Every week, people spend time and money chasing new customers. But what happens to the person who asked for a quote on Saturday, got busy, and never heard back? Tomorrow we're building a simple system so those opportunities don't vanish into the void.

## Your mission

Build a tiny front-office assistant that helps you:
- Remember every lead in one place
- Spot who needs your attention today
- Draft a helpful reply without inventing prices
- Track the next follow-up so nobody gets forgotten

**No coding, no new website, and no fancy automation required.**

**START THE WORKSHOP:** [Open the public GitHub starter kit](https://github.com/markgromer/Scoopos-Conduit/tree/main/workshops/2026-10-12-bow-wow-ai-assistant)

### Do this during the lesson

1. Open **START-HERE.md** and download the fictional customer example CSV.
2. Import it into a new Airtable base or Google Sheets.
3. Copy the **BUSINESS-BRAIN.md** questions and fill in what is actually true about your business.
4. Run the first three prompts in **PROMPTS.md**.
5. Pick one lead and write down the next action and follow-up date.

Don't get stuck connecting everything. If your ChatGPT account can't access Airtable, use the CSV upload method and keep going. You will still learn the workflow.

### 24-hour challenge

Find 3 customers or prospects in your own private records whose next step is unclear. Where you're permitted to contact them, draft one thoughtful follow-up and review it before using your normal business communication channel.

**Bring back one win:** "I found an opportunity I might have forgotten, and I know what to do next."

*This is the beginner framework, not a live Facebook-to-ChatGPT automation or an auto-texting bot. We're building the useful habit first.*
