# Bow Wow Workshop: Build Your First AI Business Assistant
**ScoopOS / Poop Scoop Syndicate | October 12, 2026 | 4:00 PM Arizona time**

**Today's win:** Build a lead board, teach ChatGPT your business rules, and work one prospect from "new lead" to "next action." No coding, domain, API key, paid automation, or GitHub account required.

> **Start here:** [5-minute setup](START-HERE.md) · [Live lesson](LESSON-60-MINUTES.md) · [Copy/paste prompts](PROMPTS.md) · [Hands-on practice](practice/index.html)

## What you are building

**Customer inquiry → Lead board → AI-assisted review → Human-approved reply → Follow-up due date → Result recorded**

- **Airtable** (or a spreadsheet) is the lead board: where the facts live.
- **ChatGPT** is the assistant: prioritizes work, drafts replies, and helps you decide the next step.
- **You** approve outbound communication, prices, and record changes.
- **GitHub** is just the classroom toolbox. You do NOT need to learn Git.

This is the **manual-first starter**. It does **not** automatically read Facebook leads, send texts, call customers, or synchronize Sweep & Go. Those require approved integrations that are not part of this workshop. LeadBridge is **not shipped**.

## Pick your path

### Beginner (recommended, about 20 minutes)
1. Download [demo leads CSV](data/demo-leads.csv) or [empty lead tracker CSV](data/blank-leads.csv).
2. [Import it](START-HERE.md) into a new Airtable base (use a desktop browser) or Google Sheets.
3. Fill in [your business brain](BUSINESS-BRAIN.md).
4. Copy [Prompt 1](PROMPTS.md#prompt-1-set-up-your-assistant), then [Prompt 2](PROMPTS.md#prompt-2-find-todays-best-opportunities) into ChatGPT.
5. Draft a reply for one fictional lead and record the follow-up yourself. If your Airtable connector is available, you can optionally approve a specific record update.

### No Airtable connector?
You can use the **same** CSV in Google Sheets or upload/paste the fictional rows into ChatGPT. Make updates manually. You'll still finish the lesson.

### Want more later?
Read [What's next, and what's not live yet](NEXT-LEVEL.md). Don't turn the first 20 minutes into six login screens and an accidental software engineering career.
